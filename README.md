📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Electric Vehicle market performance, sales trends, vehicle categories, battery technologies, manufacturers, and customer ratings through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard was created using Tableau Public with a sample dataset containing 40 records.

📊 Dataset Information

- Dataset: EV Market Sample Data
- Number of Records: 40
- Time Period: 2021–2025
- Total EV Sales Units: 3,420,000
- Tools Used: Tableau Public and Microsoft Excel

Dataset Fields

- Year
- Country
- Manufacturer
- Vehicle Category
- Battery Technology
- Annual Sales Units
- Driving Range (KM)
- Customer Rating
- Price (INR)

📈 Key Performance Indicators

- Total EV Sales: 3,420,000 units
- Average Driving Range: 429.3 KM
- Average Customer Rating: 4.265

📊 Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards showing total EV sales, average driving range, and average customer rating
- Year-wise EV Sales Trend
- EV Sales by Vehicle Category
- EV Sales by Battery Technology
- EV Sales by Country
- EV Sales by Manufacturer
- Customer Rating vs Average Vehicle Price
- Global EV Sales by Country Map
- Interactive Year Filter
- Tableau Story for presenting project insights

🔍 Key Insights

- EV sales show an increasing trend from 2021 to 2025 in the sample dataset.
- SUV is the highest-selling vehicle category in the dashboard.
- NMC battery technology has higher sales than LFP in the dataset.
- The dashboard compares EV sales across different countries and manufacturers.
- Customer ratings and average vehicle prices are analyzed together.
